Something I wanted to tell you

My Harini,

This is your fourth birthday after we got married.

When I look back at these years, there is something I have wanted to tell you from my heart for a long time.

I am truly sorry.

I know I have given you pain when I should have given you peace. I have hurt you many times. There were moments when my words and my actions made you feel alone, unwanted, and even made you feel like you should walk away from me.

And I know there were times when I failed to understand your feelings. I should have listened to you. I should have stood beside you. I should have made you feel that you were never alone in this marriage.

Instead, there were situations involving my family where I allowed things to affect our relationship, and I did not handle them the way I should have. That was my failure.

I cannot go back and change those moments.

I cannot erase the tears I caused.

I cannot take back the words that hurt you.

But I can do one thing.

I can change what comes next.

I want you to know that I don't want our future to be a continuation of our painful past. I want it to be something we build together — with understanding, respect, peace, love, and trust.

You and our baby deserve a husband and father who makes you feel safe, valued, loved, and supported.

I promise I will keep working to become that person.

I cannot promise that I will never make a mistake again. But I can promise that I will listen better, understand better, protect our relationship better, and never stop trying to give you the life and happiness you deserve.

The past is past. I cannot go back and fix it.

But I can fix our future.

And I want to spend the rest of my life doing that with you.

I am sorry for every time I hurt you.

I am sorry for every time I failed to understand you.

And most of all, I am sorry for making the person I love feel that she had to go away from me.

Thank you for still being here.

I love you, Harini.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️

— Rohin
